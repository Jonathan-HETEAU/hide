BoundsClipping

DebugView

LightProbe

A local environment lighting in a volume (its unit box), from a texture or a capture of the scene.

LightProbeObject

The mesh of a LightProbe: applies its indirect lighting to the pixels inside it.

ProbeBaker

Captures the environment around a position and computes its spherical harmonics, for the volumetric lightmaps.

ProbeFadeMode

The fade of a LightProbe near its bounds.

ProbeMode

Where the environment of a LightProbe comes from.

Shape

The shape of a LightProbe.

SphericalHarmonic

The spherical harmonic coefficients of a color.

VolumetricLightmap

A volumetric lightmap: a grid of light probes, baked as spherical harmonics in the editor, giving the indirect lighting inside its volume.

VolumetricMesh

The mesh of a VolumetricLightmap: applies the lighting of its probes to the pixels inside its unit box.
