hrt.prefab.terrain

..
Direction

A direction in the grid of tiles (not used).

NormalBake

Computes a normal map from a height map.

PackWeight

Packs the weight textures of the surfaces into an index map and a packed weight texture (the strongest surfaces of each pixel).

Surface

A material of the terrain: textures with their placement and height blending.

SurfaceArray

The textures of all the surfaces, in texture arrays, with their parameters.

SurfaceProps

The serialized properties of a surface.

Terrain

A terrain made of tiles, with height maps and painted surfaces, editable in the editor. Its textures are saved as binary files next to the prefab.

TerrainCopy

Copies a texture into a part of another, to resize the tiles.

TerrainMesh

The object of a Terrain: its tiles and surfaces.

TerrainResize

Resizes the tiles of a terrain, resampling their textures.

Tile

A tile of a terrain, with its height map, normal map and surface weight maps.

UnpackWeight

Extracts the weight texture of a surface from the index map and the packed weight texture.
