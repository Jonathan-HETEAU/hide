Align

The orientation of the particles of a GPUEmitter.

BaseSimulation

The base simulation of the GPU particles: life, speed, size and orientation.

BaseSpawn

The base spawn of the GPU particles: life, random value and initial speed.

CameraWrap

Wraps the particles in the cube around the camera (Camera mode).

ComputeUtils

The base of the compute shaders of the GPU emitter, with random and matrix helpers.

CubeSpawn

Spawns the particles of a GPU emitter in a cube.

CubeSpawnShader

Spawns the particles in a box.

Data

The parameters of a GPUEmitterObject.

DiskSpawn

Spawns the particles of a GPU emitter on a horizontal disk.

DiskSpawnShader

Spawns the particles on a disk.

GPUEmitter

A particle emitter simulated on the GPU with compute shaders. Its children are the particles (one GPUEmitterObject per mesh), and its SpawnShader and SimulationShader children customize the spawn and the simulation.

GPUEmitterObject

The object of a GPUEmitter for a particle mesh: a h3d.scene.MeshBatch updated on the GPU.

GravitySimulation

Applies a gravity to the particles of a GPU emitter.

GravitySimulationShader

Applies the gravity to the particles.

LocalRotation

Rotates the particles of a GPU emitter around random axes over their life.

LocalRotationShader

Rotates the particles over their life.

MeshSpawn

Spawns the particles of a GPU emitter on the vertices of its child skinned mesh (a sphere if none).

MeshSpawnShader

Spawns the particles on the vertices of a mesh.

Mode

The space of the particles of a GPUEmitter.

OrbitSpeedSimulation

Makes the particles of a GPU emitter orbit around an axis through the emitter.

OrbitSpeedSimulationShader

Moves the particles around the emitter.

ParticleShader

Reads the particle data (life, random value, color) in the rendering of a GPU particle.

PreviousModelViewSimulation

Stores the previous transform of the particles, for the motion vectors.

RandomColorSpawn

Gives the particles of a GPU emitter a random color between two colors.

RandomColorSpawnShader

Gives the particles a random color.

RelativeTransformSimulation

Applies a transform (position, scale, rotation, speed) to the particles of a GPU emitter.

RelativeTransformSimulationShader

Applies a transform to the particles.

SimulationShader

A shader prefab added to the simulation of its parent GPUEmitter.

SpawnShader

A shader prefab added to the spawn of its parent GPUEmitter.

SpeedMode

The initial speed of the particles of a GPUEmitter.

SphereSpawn

Spawns the particles of a GPU emitter in a sphere shell, between two radiuses.

SphereSpawnShader

Spawns the particles in a sphere.

Template

A child of a GPUEmitter used as particle.

TurbulenceSimulation

Applies a noise turbulence to the particles of a GPU emitter.

TurbulenceSimulationShader

Applies a noise turbulence to the speed of the particles.

UpdateParamShader

Updates an animated shader parameter of the particles from the baked animation texture.
