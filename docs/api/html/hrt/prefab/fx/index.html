Emit2DShape

EmitShape

The shape in which the particles are emitted.

EmitType

When the particles are emitted.

Emitter

A particle emitter of an FX: emits instances of its child prefab (the particle template) with curves for the parameters (children curves named after the parameters).

Emitter2D

A 2D particle emitter of an FX2D. Its parameters are stored in props, and can be animated by children curves named after them.

Emitter2DObject

The 2D object of an Emitter2D: simulates and renders the particles (with a h2d.SpriteBatch). Its fields are the evaluated parameters of the prefab.

EmitterHelper

Helpers for the emitter parameters.

EmitterMacros

Builds the parameters of Emitter from their @:param and @:instParam metadata.

EmitterObject

The 3D object of an Emitter: simulates and renders the particles (with a h3d.scene.MeshBatch). Its fields are the evaluated parameters of the prefab.

Evaluator

Evaluates Values, with random values per particle and the FX parameters.

Event

An event of an FX timeline, played between time and time + duration. Events named loop and end set the loop of the FX.

Event3D

A 3D object event of an FX timeline.

EventInstance

A playing event.

FX

A 3D FX: a timeline of animations, particles and events. Its children under a prefab named FXRoot are made at runtime.

FX2D

A 2D FX: a timeline of animations, particles and events.

FX2DAnimation

The 2D object of an FX2D: plays its animations, emitters and events.

FXAnimation

The 3D object of an FX: plays its animations, emitters, events and sub FX.

FXPlayState

What part of the FXAnimation loop is currently playing

IEvent

An event of an FX timeline.

InstanceDef

The values of the particle parameters, evaluated for each particle.

LookAt

Makes its children face a target object, or the camera.

LookAtObject

The object of a LookAt.

ObjectAnimation

The animated transform, color and visibility of an object of an FX, from the curves of its prefab.

Orientation

The initial orientation of the emitted particles.

ParamDef

ParameterType

ShaderTargetObj

Single

SpriteType

SubEmitterKind

When a sub emitter (child emitter) is spawned for a particle.

SubFX

Plays a referenced FX as an event of an FX.

Swarm

A swarm of objects (its first visible child, or small boxes) moving toward targets rotating on a sphere around it.

SwarmElement

An object of a Swarm.

SwarmObject

The object of a Swarm: simulates the objects at 15 steps per second and renders them (with a h3d.scene.MeshBatch).

Value

An animated value of an FX: constant, curve, random, or combination. Below, random(rid) is the random value rid of the particle, between -1 and 1.

Vector
