AutoExposure

Adapts the exposure of the PBR renderer to the direction of the camera relative to the main directional light.

Bloom

Adds a glow around the bright pixels.

BloomTonemap

Adds the bloom texture in the tonemapping.

Border

Draws a colored border around the screen.

BorderShader

Draws a colored border around the screen.

ChromaticAberration

Shifts the red and blue channels toward the screen borders, as a lens would.

ChromaticAberrationShader

Shifts the red and blue channels toward the screen borders.

CloudShadow

Projects the shadows of scrolling clouds with the main directional light.

ColorGrading

Transforms the colors with a lookup table texture, in the tonemapping.

ColorGradingFunc

Applies a color lookup table.

ColorGradingTonemap

Applies the lookup table in the tonemapping.

ColorGradingTonemapBlend

Applies a blend of two lookup tables in the tonemapping, for transitions.

Configurator

Runs a script each frame to change properties of the renderer and prefabs, depending on variables. The script can use the variables and the functions get and getOpt (a prefab by path), getParts, smooth, mix, mixColor and allowChanges. The changes are restored after the frame.

ConfiguratorInterp

The script interpreter of a Configurator: remembers the fields it changes, to restore them after the frame.

DirLightWithClouds

A directional light attenuated by a scrolling cloud texture.

DistanceBlur

Blurs the near and far parts of the screen (depth of field).

DistanceBlurShader

Blends the blurred screen depending on the distance.

Distortion

Distorts the screen with the objects drawn in the distortion pass.

DistortionTonemap

Offsets the texture coordinates of the tonemapping by the distortion map.

DualFilterDown

Downscales the bloom (dual filtering).

DualFilterUp

Upscales the bloom (dual filtering).

FrustumJitter

Cycles through the sub-pixel offsets of a pattern, to jitter the camera frustum each frame (temporal anti-aliasing).

GenFog

A fog depending on the distance and the height, with a color gradient and a light scattering toward the shadow light.

GenFogBlendShader

Renders a blend of two GenFog fogs, for transitions.

GenFogFunc

The functions computing the fog of GenFog.

GenFogNoise

The noise displacing the fog.

GenFogRenderMode

When the fog of GenFog is rendered.

GenFogShader

Renders the fog of GenFog.

HierarchicalSSR

Hierarchical screen space reflections (h3d.pass.SSR). Not rendered in the editor.

KuwaharaFilter

A Kuwahara filter, giving a painting look, with an opacity depending on the distance.

KuwaharaShader

Applies a Kuwahara filter (painting effect), depending on the distance.

LightVolume

Renders the light volumes (hrt.prefab.pbr.LightVolume) drawn in the lightVolume pass.

Outline

Draws an outline around the objects drawn in the highlight passes.

Pattern

A pattern of sub-pixel offsets of the camera, for temporal anti-aliasing.

Refraction

Renders the objects drawn in the refraction pass with a refraction of the scene behind them.

RefractionShader

Renders the refracting surfaces, sampling the screen displaced by their normal.

RendererFX

The base of the renderer effect prefabs: a h3d.impl.RendererFX added to the renderer. The prefab is cloned as the effect instance.

SSR

Screen space reflections, on the surfaces with a low roughness (or marked by the ssrNormalMask pass).

SSRShader

Ray marches the reflections in screen space.

Sao

Scalable ambient occlusion: a screen space ambient occlusion, combined with the material occlusion.

SaoBlendMode

How the screen space occlusion is combined with the material occlusion.

SaoMerge

Combines the screen space occlusion with the material occlusion.

ScreenShaderGraph

A screen effect made with a shader graph (source). Its parameters are stored in props.

ScreenShaderGraphMode

When a ScreenShaderGraph is rendered.

Sharpen

Sharpens the screen.

SharpenShader

Sharpens the screen.

TemporalBloom

A bloom with dual filtering and a temporal filter, more stable than Bloom.

TemporalFiltering

Temporal anti-aliasing: jitters the camera each frame and blends with the previous frames.

TemporalFilteringShader

Blends the frame with the previous ones.

Threshold

Keeps the bright pixels, with a temporal filter.

Vignetting

Colors the screen borders.

VignettingShader

Colors the screen borders.

VolumetricLighting

A volumetric fog lit by the lights (with their shadows), computed by ray marching at half resolution.

VolumetricLightingMode

The lights of VolumetricLighting.

VolumetricLightingShader

Ray marches the fog and the lights of VolumetricLighting.
